Popularity, confidence-weighted momentum, acceleration, relative growth, freshness, maintenance, official GitHub community-health evidence, adoption depth, metadata quality, catalog relevance, descriptive novelty, under-recognition, confidence, and newness remain separate dimensions. Review flags identify incomplete or unusual evidence; they are not findings of manipulation or misconduct.
