# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a constructive, evidence-oriented community in which people can contribute regardless of background, identity, experience level, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss technical claims with evidence and respect;
- disclose project affiliations when making nominations or recommendations;
- distinguish criticism of a method from criticism of a person;
- welcome corrections and uncertainty;
- avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, or unwanted sexual attention;
- avoid spam, coordinated ranking manipulation, and undisclosed promotion;
- respect privacy, platform rules, licenses, and responsible disclosure practices.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, nominations, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent participation restrictions.

Report conduct concerns privately to `amarel.taylor.s@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and assessed in context.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public interactions where someone is representing the project or its community.
