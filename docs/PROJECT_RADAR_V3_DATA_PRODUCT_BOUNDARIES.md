# Project Radar v3 data-product boundaries

V3 exports support public research, discovery, monitoring, and downstream tooling. They do not grant rights beyond the licenses and terms of upstream sources.

Consumers should preserve source attribution, observation timestamps, evidence classes, provider scope, and missing-data semantics. Cross-registry download values should not be compared as though they share one counting method. Generated profiles and inferred graph relationships must remain labeled as interpretation or configured association.

The public snapshot contains no private subscriber records. Optional commercial services may build hosting, alerts, analytics, custom catalogs, APIs, or workflow integrations around the open dataset, but paid placement must not alter the public ranking and private customer data must remain separate from the public corpus.
