# Project Radar v3 editorial independence

Measured leaderboards, experimental shadow scores, generated profiles, sponsored material, and human editorial selections are separate products.

Human-curated collections may explain why projects matter, compare alternatives, or highlight use cases, but they must be labeled and must not overwrite measured evidence or silently change automated ranking. Generated interpretations retain their source bundles and template version. Corrections to factual metadata follow the evidence workflow rather than editorial preference.

Maintainers, sponsors, nominators, and benchmark contributors disclose affiliations. Public rankings remain reproducible from the versioned source snapshot and methodology.
